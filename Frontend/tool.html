  • OSS / BSS Platforms: Used for network operations, billing, service provisioning, and customer lifecycle management.
  • Real-Time Charging Systems: Instantly calculate and charge customers for voice, SMS, data, and digital services.
  • 5G Core Networks: Power next-generation ultra-fast, low-latency mobile networks.
  • Policy Control & Traffic Management: Control data usage, bandwidth allocation, and service priority.
  • IoT Connectivity Platforms: Manage millions of connected devices across industries.

  • Backend: Java, Python, Go
  • Messaging: Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ
  • Databases: PostgreSQL, Oracle, MongoDB, Cassandra
  • APIs: RESTful APIs, GraphQL
  • Containers: Docker, Kubernetes
🔹 How These Tools Are Used
  • Activating SIM cards and digital services within seconds.
  • Handling prepaid and postpaid billing systems for millions of users.
  • Monitoring telecom networks 24/7 to ensure service quality.
  • Supporting roaming, interconnect, and partner ecosystems.
