About Comviva

Comviva is a global leader in digital solutions for telecom and fintech, enabling businesses to innovate, transform, and grow in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

Our platforms power customer engagement, payments, messaging, digital commerce, and enterprise transformation across emerging and mature markets worldwide.

Rajesh Chandiramani

Designation: Whole-time Director & Chief Executive Officer

Rajesh Chandiramani is the Chief Executive Officer at Comviva, bringing over 30 years of comprehensive experience in spearheading global programs and initiatives across diverse organizations.

Prior to joining Comviva, he played a pivotal role at Tech Mahindra, where he served as the Business Unit Head for strategic markets spanning the UK, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific, Japan, and India within the communications, media, and entertainment vertical.

He has also steered global digital businesses, overseeing cloud, data and analytics, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and blockchain technologies, and led APAC enterprise growth across diverse verticals.

An alumnus of prestigious institutions such as Harvard Business School and Michigan University, Rajesh is esteemed for his leadership acumen, strategic vision, and unwavering commitment to driving digital impact.

Prashant Pandey

Designation: Vice President

With over 18 years of extensive experience in managing customers spanning Asia, the Middle East, and Africa in the telecom industry, Prashant Pandey is a seasoned executive with a blend of technical and customer management expertise.

His leadership acumen, complemented by hands-on network experience, has driven the successful delivery of mission-critical projects across diverse ecosystems.

He specializes in providing strategic direction in telecom, IT & products, transformation, innovation, and operational governance within complex network environments.
